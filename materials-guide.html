The Makrana Marble & Noble Stone Guide

Understanding pure Makrana calcite: why the Taj Mahal and royal sanctums built from this geological wonder remain pure white for centuries without degradation.

Geological Superiority

99% Pure Calcite Structure

Unlike commercial imported marbles or dolomitic stones containing high levels of iron and magnesium, authentic Makrana marble quarried from Nagaur district, Rajasthan is composed of 98% to 99% pure calcium carbonate calcite crystals.

  • Zero Porosity Absorption: Will not absorb milk, honey, sandalwood paste, or oil during sacred puja rituals.
  • Self-Polishing Luster: The more Makrana marble is touched and washed, the more brilliant its diamond-crystalline glow becomes over generations.
  • Zero Chemical Resins: Cut directly from monolithic bedrock without epoxy mesh backing or synthetic resin hardening.

Makrana Calcite

99% calcite crystal matrix. 0.04% water absorption. Zero yellowing over centuries. Certified for sacred pooja abhishekam.

Commercial Dolomite

High magnesium content with 1-3% open porosity. Tends to absorb oils, water, and turns dull yellow within 5-10 years.

Engineered Quartz

Composed of 7-10% synthetic resin binders. Prone to scorching under diya lamp heat and forbidden in classical Shilpa Shastra.
