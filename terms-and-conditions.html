Terms & Conditions of Custom Commission

Clear, transparent protocols governing bespoke architectural sculpting, milestone approvals, ISPM-15 crating, and international transit.

1. Bespoke Commission Scope & Sign-Off

Every marble temple, multi-tier water fountain, chhatri gazebo, and pietra dura inlay crafted by A.F.G Marble & Arts is a custom commissioned work of art. Work commences upon mutual sign-off on:

• Detailed 3D CAD elevation blueprint with verified site dimensions
• Block selection report (high-resolution quarry photos of raw Makrana or imported blocks)
• Formally agreed stone type and gold foil/inlay specifications

2. Natural Stone Variations & Authenticity

Natural marble is an authentic geological formation created over millions of years. Subtle calcite crystal clouds, tonal variations, and mineral veins are authentic hallmarks of genuine Makrana marble and not structural defects.

3. Milestone Schedule & Workshop Dry-Fit

To ensure absolute peace of mind for distant and international patrons, every temple and heavy fountain undergoes:

• Weekly high-definition video and photo dispatches from our carving sheds
• Full dry-fit trial assembly inside our Jaipur atelier prior to crating
• Joint alignment and numbering system verification with live video inspection

4. Export Packaging, ISPM-15 Crating & Transit Insurance

All shipments dispatched across India or exported internationally to the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, and Australia are packaged in heavy-duty, heat-treated, fumigated ISPM-15 compliant export timber crates. Every consignment is protected with comprehensive all-risk marine and overland cargo insurance.

5. On-Site Erection & Supervision

For patrons requesting master artisan deployment, A.F.G Marble & Arts dispatches certified senior craftsmen to supervise anchoring, joint leveling, and sealant finishing on-site at your residence or commercial estate.
