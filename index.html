Frequently Answered Queries

Everything You Need to Know

Can temples and heavy marble fountains be shipped safely internationally? ↓

Yes, absolutely. Over 40% of our commissions are exported to royal and residential patrons in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, and Singapore. Every piece is precision-numbered, vacuum-sealed in moisture barriers, and enclosed inside heavy-duty, fumigated ISPM-15 export wooden crates. Full transit insurance is provided door-to-door.

How is authentic Makrana marble distinguished from commercial market marble? ↓

Authentic Makrana marble has a 99% calcium carbonate calcite structure with zero open porosity. Unlike common commercial marbles or composite stones that absorb water and turn yellow within 5-10 years, pure Makrana marble never yellows, does not absorb oil or milk during puja abhishekam, and retains its pure white crystal shine for generations.

How are Vastu Shastra principles incorporated into custom mandir designs? ↓

Our temple architects collaborate directly with your family pandit or our in-house Vastu consultants. We calculate the Ayadi coordinates to ensure auspicious heights, widths, pedestal depths, and sanctum orientations (ideal North-East Ishanya placement) before hand-chiseling begins.

What is the typical timeline for commissioning a bespoke temple or fountain? ↓

Depending on carving complexity and dimensions, an exclusive bespoke home mandir or palatial tiered fountain takes between 4 to 8 weeks. Throughout this journey, patrons receive regular photo and 4K video updates documenting each stage of hand-chiseling.

Does A.F.G Marble & Arts assist with on-site assembly and installation? ↓

Yes. For major domestic and international projects, our master craftsmen travel directly to the client's estate to supervise stone assembly, mortar anchoring, and precision alignment. For smaller commissions, we provide comprehensive step-by-step numbered diagrams and video guidance.

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: October 2026 · A.F.G Marble & Arts

1. Scope of Bespoke Handcraft & Commissions

All temples, fountains, chhatris, murtis, and architectural elements created by A.F.G Marble & Arts ("The Atelier") are custom-commissioned and sculpted by hand. Each creation is inherently unique and produced in accordance with client-approved dimensions and specifications.

2. Natural Stone Characteristics & Mineral Variations

Natural stones—including Makrana White marble, Calacatta Oro, Botticino Classico, Ambaji marble, and granite—are natural geological formations. Inherent crystalline veining, subtle shade transitions, and micro-fossils are authentic hallmarks of genuine earth-hewn stone and are not considered defects.

3. Architectural CAD Sign-Off & Approvals

Prior to physical stone chiseling, detailed 2D/3D architectural CAD drawings will be submitted to the client. Physical stone cutting begins exclusively upon formal client sign-off. Any post-approval structural alterations requested by the client may incur additional material and artisan carving costs.

4. Dry-Fit Assembly & Quality Inspection

For modular temples and tiered fountains, a full dry-fit pre-assembly is conducted in our Rajasthan atelier prior to packaging. The client receives comprehensive high-resolution photographic and video documentation for final approval before crating.

5. International Shipping, Crating & Transit Insurance

All items dispatched domestically or internationally are crated in compliant, fumigated ISPM-15 export wooden crates with high-density shock-absorbing padding. International consignments are shipped under standard Incoterms (CIF or DDU) with full transit insurance against damage or loss.

6. Payment Terms & Production Milestones

Commissions typically follow a milestone schedule: initial design and quarry block reservation deposit (40%), progress carving milestone (30%), and balance payment prior to crating and dispatch (30%). Custom deposits are non-refundable once quarry block excavation has begun.

7. Governing Jurisdiction

Any disputes arising under these terms shall be subject exclusively to the jurisdiction of the courts of Jaipur / Nagaur District (Makrana), Rajasthan, India.
