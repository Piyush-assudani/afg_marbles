Frequently Answered Queries

Everything You Need to Know

Can temples and heavy marble fountains be shipped safely internationally? ↓

Yes, absolutely. Over 40% of our commissions are exported to royal and residential patrons in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, and Singapore. Every piece is precision-numbered, vacuum-sealed in moisture barriers, and enclosed inside heavy-duty, fumigated ISPM-15 export wooden crates. Full transit insurance is provided door-to-door.

How is authentic Makrana marble distinguished from commercial market marble? ↓

Authentic Makrana marble has a 99% calcium carbonate calcite structure with zero open porosity. Unlike common commercial marbles or composite stones that absorb water and turn yellow within 5-10 years, pure Makrana marble never yellows, does not absorb oil or milk during puja abhishekam, and retains its pure white crystal shine for generations.

How are Vastu Shastra principles incorporated into custom mandir designs? ↓

Our temple architects collaborate directly with your family pandit or our in-house Vastu consultants. We calculate the Ayadi coordinates to ensure auspicious heights, widths, pedestal depths, and sanctum orientations (ideal North-East Ishanya placement) before hand-chiseling begins.

What is the typical timeline for commissioning a bespoke temple or fountain? ↓

Depending on carving complexity and dimensions, an exclusive bespoke home mandir or palatial tiered fountain takes between 4 to 8 weeks. Throughout this journey, patrons receive regular photo and 4K video updates documenting each stage of hand-chiseling.

Does A.F.G Marble & Arts assist with on-site assembly and installation? ↓

Yes. For major domestic and international projects, our master craftsmen travel directly to the client's estate to supervise stone assembly, mortar anchoring, and precision alignment. For smaller commissions, we provide comprehensive step-by-step numbered diagrams and video guidance.

Terms & Conditions of Custom Commission

1. Bespoke Commission Scope

Every marble temple, water fountain, chhatri, and architectural element sculpted by A.F.G Marble & Arts is a custom, commissioned work of art. Final dimensions, stone selection, and design blueprints are confirmed with client sign-off prior to quarry cutting.

2. Natural Stone Characteristics

Authentic natural marble (including Makrana White, Calacatta Oro, Botticino Classico, and Nero Marquina) is an organic geological material. Natural crystalline veining, subtle tonal shifts, and calcite clouds are organic hallmarks of genuine stone authenticity and not defects.

3. Milestone Schedule & Dry-Fit Sign-Off

Production milestones include: (a) 3D CAD architectural approval, (b) raw block selection, (c) hand-chiseling updates with high-resolution photo/video dispatches, and (d) full workshop dry-fit assembly approval prior to export crating.

4. Export Crating & Transit Insurance

All items dispatched domestically or internationally are vacuum-sealed in moisture barriers and packed inside heavy-duty, fumigated ISPM-15 compliant export wooden crates. Door-to-door comprehensive transit insurance covers all maritime, air, and overland logistics.

5. On-Site Assembly & Supervision

For commissioned projects requiring on-site supervision, A.F.G Marble & Arts coordinates master artisan deployment to supervise anchoring, alignment, and joint sealing at the patron's estate.

Client Confidentiality & Privacy Policy

1. Discretion & High-Profile Patron Confidentiality

A.F.G Marble & Arts maintains strict non-disclosure standards for royal residences, private estates, and luxury villa patrons. Architectural plans, estate locations, and personal contact details are never shared or published without explicit written patron consent.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only necessary project information submitted via our consultation forms: name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address, project dimensions, and design requirements.

3. Usage of Consultation Data

Collected details are strictly utilized to prepare custom 3D CAD proposals, cost estimates, structural feasibility analyses, and logistics coordination. We do not sell or lease client contact records to third parties.

4. Digital Communication & WhatsApp Privacy

Inquiries transmitted via WhatsApp or direct email are processed through encrypted end-to-end channels directly to our lead master artisan desk in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
