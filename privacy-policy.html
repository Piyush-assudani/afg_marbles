Privacy Policy & Non-Disclosure

Safeguarding patron confidentiality, architectural blueprints, and private estate installations chiseled by A.F.G Marble & Arts.

1. High-Profile Patron Discretion & Non-Disclosure

A.F.G Marble & Arts operates with the highest standards of discretion for our royal, governmental, and private estate clientele worldwide. We respect that sacred home sanctums and palatial outdoor landscape installations are deeply personal family spaces.

We guarantee that architectural elevations, CAD blueprints, spatial floor plans, estate addresses, and transaction details will remain strictly confidential and will never be shared, publicized, or sold without explicit, formal written consent from the commissioning patron.

2. Information Collected During Consultation

When you submit an atelier consultation inquiry or message our master stone architects via WhatsApp, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill your design proposal:

• Patron name and honorific
• WhatsApp number and phone contact for logistics updates
• Email address for CAD drawings and invoice dispatch
• Dimensional requirements (height, width, depth) and stone preferences
• Delivery port/city for specialized ISPM-15 export crating

3. Secure Architectural CAD & 3D Render Data

All proprietary 3D CAD files and custom vastu measurements formulated for your temple or fountain are stored on secure encrypted servers accessible exclusively by our senior structural engineers in Jaipur and Makrana.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Logistics Partners

We do not sell, rent, or lease client data under any circumstances. Client delivery details are shared exclusively with certified international freight logistics partners (insured ocean container and air freight carriers) solely for customs clearance and on-site crane offloading.

For questions regarding custom non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) prior to architectural blueprinting, please contact our atelier office:
