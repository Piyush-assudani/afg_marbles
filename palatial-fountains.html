Monumental Multi-Tier Cascades

Sculptural Mastery

Classical Lion Spouts & Parterre Basins

Hand-carved lion mascarons, mythical dolphins, and lotus petals sculpted directly into monolithic stone tiers. Each spout produces a distinct acoustic pitch, filling gardens with soothing natural music.

  • Precision Spout Carving: Solid brass or bronze interior pipe linings to prevent mineral staining on white marble.
  • Custom Basin Coping: Segmented curved pool borders with bullnose edge profiles and waterproofing channels.
  • Full Workshop Wet-Testing: Every fountain is assembled and pump-tested with live water circulation in Jaipur before dispatch.
